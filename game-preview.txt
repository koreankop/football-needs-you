GAMEPLAY FLOW:
═════════════

1. Player clicks on LEFT/CENTER/RIGHT section
2. Ball animates toward chosen direction ⚽ →
3. Goalkeeper randomly dives 🧤 (left/center/right)
4. Result displayed:
   ✅ GOAL! ⚽🎉 (if keeper dives different direction)
   ❌ SAVED! 🧤 (if keeper dives same direction)
5. Score updates automatically
6. Ready for next shot!

KEYBOARD CONTROLS:
═════════════════
A or ← = Shoot Left
S or ↓ = Shoot Center
D or → = Shoot Right
